# Article Catalog — basmi.co.id

Scope: 120 Indonesian-language briefs across 20 parent topics. This is a complete planning inventory, not a publication quota. Every parent topic has six distinct articles.

Domain boundaries: neutral education is planned under `/artikel/<slug>/`; existing pest and service routes retain verified commercial intent. Cannibalization control applies only within basmi.co.id. Other owned domains may publish their own complete coverage. No city, province, or region brief is included.

Catalog statistics: 120 briefs; 40 P0, 40 P1, 20 P2, and 20 P3. Wave W1 contains the 12-asset bounded first cluster; later waves are evidence-gated.

Priority legend: `P0` foundation, safety gate, or first cluster; `P1` core pest/lifecycle depth; `P2` specialist decision support; `P3` advanced, historical, or lower-frequency depth.
